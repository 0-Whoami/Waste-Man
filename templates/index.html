Which Bin Does It Go In?

Search for an item to know how to dispose of it properly.

Wet Waste (Green Bin)

Biodegradable organic matter. Used for composting.

Examples: Food scraps, fruit peels, tea leaves.

Dry Waste (Blue Bin)

Recyclable inorganic materials. Must be clean and dry.

Examples: Plastic, paper, glass, cardboard.

Hazardous Waste (Red Bin)

Toxic or infectious materials needing special handling.

Examples: Syringes, chemicals, paint, broken glass.

E-Waste (Yellow Bin)

Electronic components containing heavy metals.

Examples: Batteries, chargers, old phones, wires.
